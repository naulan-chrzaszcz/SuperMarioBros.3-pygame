# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We, contributors and maintainers, want this project to be a friendly and
harassment-free place for everyone, whatever their age, body, disability,
ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
nationality, appearance, race, religion or sexual orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers: many of us learn by making
  this game.
- Respect other points of view and give constructive feedback about the code,
  not about the person.
- Accept responsibility for your mistakes and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its community.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Insults, trolling, derogatory or discriminatory comments.
- Harassment, in public or in private, including unwanted sexual attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Any other behaviour that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies to every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions)
and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer,
[@naulan-chrzaszcz](https://github.com/naulan-chrzaszcz), through a private
message on GitHub. Every report is reviewed confidentially. Maintainers may
remove comments, commits or contributions that break this code and may
temporarily or permanently ban a contributor, depending on the severity:
a warning first, then a temporary ban, then a permanent ban for repeated or
serious violations.
